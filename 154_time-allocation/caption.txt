Principle Nº 154: Time Allocation

I was busy all week and moved nothing that actually mattered. Busy isn't the same as productive.

How you spend your hours is how your business and life turn out. Your calendar shows your real priorities.

Your turn: Open your calendar and circle the hours that don't match what you say matters most.
