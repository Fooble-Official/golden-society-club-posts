Principle Nº 164: Stress Load Balancing

Stress isn't the problem. Stress with no recovery to match it is.

Too much stress without enough recovery breaks the whole system. Watch the ratio, not just the load.

Your turn: Rate your stress and recovery right now, divide them, and write down the number.
