Principle Nº 194: Stock and Flow

Your bank balance looks fine today. That tells you nothing about next month.

Everything you build up, like savings, customers, or trust, is a stock that flows fill and drain.

Your turn: Pick one thing you're building up and write down what fills it and what drains it.
