Principle Nº 237: Cultivate Auto-Pilot

If it only happens when you feel like it, it's not a habit. It's a mood.

Rely on systems, not motivation, because motivation won't show up every day.

Your turn: Pick one habit that depends on how you feel and write down the trigger that'll run it instead.
