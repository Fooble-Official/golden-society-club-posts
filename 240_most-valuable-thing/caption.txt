Principle Nº 240: Most Valuable Thing

Your to-do list is lying to you. Some of those items matter ten times more than the rest.

The fastest way to simplify your business is to find the single most valuable thing and organize around it.

Your turn: Write down your full list, then circle the one thing your customer would say is most valuable.
