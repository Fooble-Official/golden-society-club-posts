Principle Nº 242: Forever Feature

The flashy feature gets the launch buzz. The boring one people use every week keeps them around.

Your strongest feature, offer, or promise is the one that keeps your business valuable for years.

Your turn: Ask one customer this week what single thing would make them stay forever, and write it down.
