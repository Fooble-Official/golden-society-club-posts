Principle Nº 258: Write A Memo

That meeting went in circles because nobody wrote down what was actually being decided.

Write important decisions down before you discuss them, so everyone shows up clear instead of emotional or confused.

Your turn: Pick one decision in your business and write a one-page memo before you talk about it again.
