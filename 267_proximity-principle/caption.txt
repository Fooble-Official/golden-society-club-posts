Principle Nº 267: Proximity Principle

If everyone around you plays small, small starts to feel normal.

Your environment shapes what feels possible, so spend time around people and rooms that raise your standards.

Your turn: Name one room or relationship that would stretch what you believe is possible, and how you'll get in.
