Principle Nº 274: BHAG

Most goals I see are just last year's number plus ten percent. That won't get anyone out of bed.

Set one huge, bold goal for the next 10 to 30 years that gets your whole team fired up.

Your turn: Write down the 10-year goal so big that chasing it would change how you run things.
