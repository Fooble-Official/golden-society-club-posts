Principle Nº 283: Core Competencies

Being okay at a lot of things won't build a company. Being rare at one thing might.

Build your strategy on strengths that are rare, hard to copy, and useful across more than one market.

Your turn: Name the one thing your business does that would be really hard for anyone else to copy.
