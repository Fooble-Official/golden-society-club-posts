Principle Nº 284: OKRs

If your team can't say what winning looks like this quarter, you don't have goals. You have vibes.

Line everyone up behind a few bold objectives, each with clear, measurable key results.

Your turn: Write 3 objectives for this quarter, each with one number that proves you hit it.
