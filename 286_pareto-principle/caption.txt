Principle Nº 286: The 80/20 Rule

Most of your money probably comes from a handful of customers. Your calendar doesn't reflect that.

Roughly 80 percent of your results come from about 20 percent of your efforts, customers, or products.

Your turn: Write down your top 20% of customers or tasks, then block time for them this week.
