Principle Nº 294: Monopoly 10x Thinking

Being a little better than the competition just starts a price war. You need to be way better at one thing.

Aim to be 10 times better on the one thing customers care about most, enough to own the market.

Your turn: Name the one thing you could realistically be 10 times better at than anyone else.
