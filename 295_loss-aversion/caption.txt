Principle Nº 295: Loss Aversion

People will fight harder to keep $100 than to win $100. Your marketing should know that.

People feel a loss about twice as strongly as a gain of the same size.

Your turn: Rewrite your main offer as the loss it prevents, not the gain it adds.
