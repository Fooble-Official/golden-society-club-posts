Principle Nº 301: Social Proof

When people aren't sure, they look at what people like them are doing. So show them.

When people feel unsure, they follow others, especially people who seem similar to them.

Your turn: Find one testimonial from a customer who looks exactly like your ideal buyer and put it where prospects will see it.
