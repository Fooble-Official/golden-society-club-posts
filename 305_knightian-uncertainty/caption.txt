Principle Nº 305: Unknowable Uncertainty

Your five-year forecast is a guess dressed up in a spreadsheet. Some things just can't be predicted.

When the odds are truly unknowable, stop trying to predict and start running experiments.

Your turn: Pick one thing you're trying to predict and write a small test you could run instead.
