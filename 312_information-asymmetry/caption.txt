Principle Nº 312: Information Asymmetry

Your customers can't see what you see. So when they hesitate, it's usually not about price.

In every deal, one side knows more than the other, and that gap decides how much trust there is.

Your turn: Write down the one claim customers doubt most, and one way they could verify it themselves.
