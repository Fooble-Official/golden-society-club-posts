Principle Nº 317: Path Dependence

Half the rules in your business exist because of a choice someone made years ago. Nobody remembers why.

Where you are today is shaped by past decisions that still limit what you can do now.

Your turn: Name one rule in your business that only exists because of an old decision, then decide to keep or kill it.
