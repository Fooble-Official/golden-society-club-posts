Principle Nº 319: Red Queen Effect

You're working harder than ever and still losing ground. That's because your competitors are working harder too.

In a market where everyone keeps improving, you have to keep getting faster just to stay in place.

Your turn: Count the real improvements you shipped last quarter, then count what your top competitor shipped.
