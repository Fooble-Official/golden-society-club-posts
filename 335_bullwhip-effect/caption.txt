Principle Nº 335: Bullwhip Effect

Customers bought a little more this week, and suddenly your supplier thinks it's a boom.

Small changes in customer demand turn into huge swings the further back up the supply chain you go.

Your turn: Compare last month's orders to suppliers against your actual customer sales. Note the gap.
