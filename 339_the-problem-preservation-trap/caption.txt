Principle Nº 339: The Problem-Preservation Trap

If your business makes more money when the problem sticks around, be honest with yourself about that.

Organizations quietly keep alive the very problem they were built to solve.

Your turn: Write down one recurring fee that depends on your customer's problem never fully going away.
