Principle Nº 347: Cornered Resource

Competitors can copy your offer. They can't copy the location, the contract, or the person they don't have.

Sometimes your whole advantage is one asset rivals want but simply can't get their hands on.

Your turn: Name one asset you have that a competitor would want but couldn't buy.
