Principle Nº 350: Regression to the Mean

Your best month ever probably won't repeat, and that doesn't mean you did something wrong.

Extreme results are mostly luck, and luck doesn't repeat. Expect things to drift back toward normal.

Your turn: Take your best month this year and write down what a normal month really looks like.
