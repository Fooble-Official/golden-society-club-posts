Principle Nº 351: The Aggregation Paradox

Your overall numbers look great. Your segments might be telling a completely different story.

A trend can hold in every single group and still flip once you lump the groups together.

Your turn: Take your top metric and break it down by customer type. Note whether the trend flips.
