Principle Nº 353: Real Options

You don't have to decide everything today. Sometimes paying to wait is the smart move.

Keeping your options open isn't indecision. When the future's uncertain, it's an asset you can actually put a price on.

Your turn: Pick one big decision and write down what a small deposit to wait would cost you.
