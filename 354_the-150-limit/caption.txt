Principle Nº 354: The 150 Limit

Everyone used to just know what was going on. Then the team grew and that stopped working.

You can keep about 150 real relationships in your head. Past that, you need structure, not memory.

Your turn: Count how many people your team works with regularly, and note how close you are to 150.
