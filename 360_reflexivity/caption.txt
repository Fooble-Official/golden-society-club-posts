Principle Nº 360: Reflexivity

Sometimes a market is hot only because everyone believes it's hot.

What people believe changes the actual numbers, which changes what they believe. The two spiral together.

Your turn: Write down the story everyone in your market believes right now, and what happens if it breaks.
