Principle Nº 365: Veil of Ignorance Pricing

If you'd hate being on the other side of your own policy, it's probably not fair.

Set policies you'd still accept even if you didn't know which side of them you'd end up on.

Your turn: Read your refund or cancellation policy as if you were the customer. Rewrite one line.
